FAQ

There's a story about a child who grew up without a bike. As an adult, they buy the biggest Lamborghini to compensate, but inside, they're still the kid without a bike. To heal that, they need therapy.

But if you already have a Lamborghini (or a Volkswagen), never learned to ride a bike, and now, as an adult, you actually want to, that's coaching.

No matter how powerful your Lamborghini is, if you dream about long bike rides by the lake, that dream doesn't go away on its own.

Coaching is present and future. It's goal-oriented. We work on where you are now and where you want to go, not on what happened to you in the past. If you're unsure which one you need, we can talk about it in the strategic session. Sometimes people need one, sometimes the other, sometimes both at different moments.

(I know this because I'm the person who learned to ride a bike at 35. Every Sunday in a supermarket parking lot. Just me and a 4-year-old girl, who was significantly better than I was when I first came.)

It seems like you want to try, but have doubts. That's a completely valid place to start. Book the free strategic session to get to know each other. It doesn't commit you to anything. Before we meet, you'll fill in a short form, so you don't need to prepare anything else. The first step is usually the hardest.

You don't know it, and it can also be that you are not ready. But the road is mastered by the one who walks it. One step at a time. If you're curious and a little scared, that's ready enough to try.

We talk. But not like a normal conversation.

I listen for what's underneath what you're saying, not just what you're saying. I ask questions you probably haven't asked yourself. Sometimes we slow down and sit with something uncomfortable. Sometimes things move quickly. Sometimes slowly.

You won't leave with a five-step plan I invented for you. You'll leave with more clarity than you came with, and usually something you hadn't expected to find. Because you have the answers inside you. Sometimes they're just buried deep enough that you've stopped trusting them.

People often notice a shift already after the first session. A slight shift, when something foggy becomes slightly clearer. For example a decision that felt impossible feels a little more possible or just a chaos.

A bigger change takes longer. Most people work with me for a few months. We decide together based on what's happening and what you need. There's no minimum commitment. You're not locked in.

Pricing depends on the format. Write to me or ask in the strategic session, and I'll send you the details.

What happens during sessions stays there. I don't share notes, names, or content with anyone.
