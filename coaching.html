1:1 Coaching

You want to grow into a bigger leadership role, start your own business or finally decide whether to, double your net revenue, find a job you will love, or build a life in a new country. Whatever the goal is, at some point you ask yourself: how do I get there?

It can also be that you don't have any specific goal (you might even think you should have one, but you don't) and you don't know what the next move should be, as your whole situation feels chaotic and unstable. You feel like you should have a plan and move forward, but options are infinite and nothing resonates. You do endless research with AI and all options seem fine in chat, but none of it feels right, and you come back where you started. The circle closes, and you rethink yourself every morning.

The workWhat coaching with me looks like

Most people arrive with a story they've told themselves many times. In our sessions we take that story apart: what you want or what you don't, what keeps holding you back and what can give you a nice little boost. Once that's clear, decisions stop feeling impossible. I help you find out all of it, especially when it comes to the uncomfortable parts.

FocusWhat we work on

The main goal is for you to find your way and follow it. I'm there to help you focus, support behavioural and mindset changes, and make sure you can see your North Star and follow it at your own pace, with steadiness.

The decision you keep avoiding, the relationship dynamic you can't put a name to, the version of yourself you've outgrown but don't know how to leave behind, the exhaustion that sleep can't fix, the feeling that you're performing a life instead of living it.

If a thought or an idea keeps popping up in your head, it takes your focus and your resources. You either make it happen or stop spending your time on it. I will help you make that decision.

AudienceWho this is for

  • People going after new career goals.
  • Leaders growing into bigger roles.
  • People starting their own business or side project, or seriously thinking about it. Many of them are expats building a life in a new country.

FitWe will match if

  1. You want change and don't expect the work to be done by others.
  2. You are ready to be honest with yourself, even when it's uncomfortable.
  3. You don't need to be in crisis, you need to be ready to start.
