Workshops

Workshops for teams that want to work better together

All workshops are designed around your team's specific situation. We start with a conversation about what's actually going on, and build next steps from there. No off-the-shelf content.

People & Communication

Feedback · Difficult conversations · Communication & trust · Psychological safety · Nonviolent communication & active listening · Roles & expectations

The way people talk to each other, or don't, shapes everything else. These workshops help teams build honest, direct communication habits, create safety to speak up, and have the conversations they've been avoiding. Practical, grounded, and connected to what your team actually deals with every day.

Teams & Collaboration

Team agreements · Cross-cultural & cross-generational collaboration · Conflict prevention & resolution

Good collaboration doesn't happen by default. It's built. These workshops help teams understand how they work together, where friction comes from, and how to turn differences in background, style, or expectation into something that actually strengthens the team.

Leadership

Leadership presence · Awareness & development

For leaders who want to understand their own impact more clearly and grow into their role with more confidence and intention.

Direction & Meaning

Mission & values · OKRs & goal alignment · Decision making under uncertainty

Teams do their best work when they know why they exist and where they're going. These workshops help teams and organisations connect to their purpose, set meaningful goals, and make clearer decisions, especially when things are uncertain.

Agility & Ways of Working

Agile fundamentals · Scrum & Kanban practices · Agile mindset & culture · Retrospectives · Agile for non-tech teams

Agility is not a methodology, it's a way of thinking. These workshops help teams and organisations understand what working in an agile way actually means in practice, beyond the ceremonies and the sticky notes. Whether you're introducing agile for the first time or trying to make it work better, we focus on the mindset shift that makes the practices stick.
