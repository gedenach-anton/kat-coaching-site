About me

The beginningIt started with language

I have a master's in linguistics. As a translator I realized the real translation problem is the gap between what people say, what they mean, what they want to say but don't, and what they need but can't realise.

Then I moved into IT. Teams, managers, sprints, retros, stakeholders, products, deliveries, deadlines, bottom lines, red lines... And there I noticed same patterns:

People speaking the same language still didn't understand each other. They were often saying the same thing, sharing the same opinion, and still they were arguing with each other.

The problem isn't vocabulary, nor communication skills. As a manager, I saw it on so many levels: not only do people not understand each other, they also know very little about themselves. What they need, what they want, what they're afraid of, or what they really want to say but don't.

That's when I understood what kind of translation actually matters and what I really, deeply want to do.

PracticeHow I work

Our work is all about focus, goals and state of mind. Whether you want to build a business, change career, scale up, balance your work with family or a time-consuming hobby, or reach any other goal, I will help you build the system that works for you, and I won't let you hide from yourself.

This is a space without judgment, any topic is welcome, no issue is too small, no problem too unimportant. If something is sitting with you, it deserves attention, regardless of whether it looks "serious enough" from the outside. The energy your brain wastes on ignoring or avoiding this topic, after being explored and understood, can be used to achieve your real goals.

You won't get unsolicited advice or a to-do list from me, but with my help, you will learn to hear yourself more clearly: what's actually going on underneath the story you've been telling, the pressure you've been carrying, the version of yourself you've been performing.

Once you see the obstacles on your way, we will remove them or get through together.

It might look “just talking”, but it's work, sometimes uncomfortable, surprising and sweaty.

TrainingEducation & approach

I am a certified systemic coach and trained mediator in Berlin. I keep learning, drawing on approaches from ICF, IFS, NLP, CBT and other 3-4 letter abbreviations. I am sure there is no one-size-fits-all approach, and each new layer of knowledge gives me a new angle or perspective.

I am truly, deeply passionate about it, so don't ask me what I am studying now if you don't have two spare hours.

BackgroundA bit more about me

I've lived in Ukraine, Israel, and Germany. Moving from country to country, often without a plan B, taught me what it means to rebuild yourself in a new context: new language, new environment, new version of who you are vs who you're supposed to be.

I work in English, Ukrainian, Russian, French, and German (if you don't judge my articles). Based in Berlin, online with people worldwide.

My background combines linguistics, IT and Product, agile teams, leadership, coaching, and mediation.

But honestly, none of that matters as much as whether the work together feels right for you.
