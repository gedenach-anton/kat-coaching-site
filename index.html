Berlin · online worldwide

I've been a linguist, a translator, a product owner in fintech and a team lead before I became a coach, mediator and facilitator in Berlin. It sounds like a career without a plan, but it was one question the whole time: why do people who want the same thing still end up working against each other, and why do we so rarely know what we actually want ourselves? I listen to what isn't being said, I ask the questions nobody in the room dares to ask, and I don't let you, or your team, take the comfortable way around.
