About

I started as a linguist.
I ended up here.

I have a master's in linguistics. For years I translated — between languages, between people, between worlds. Then I moved into IT teams and leadership. And I noticed something that stopped surprising me completely.

Read more →

People who spoke the same language still didn't understand each other. Sometimes they were in a conflict — and actually saying the same thing. The problem wasn't vocabulary. It wasn't even communication skills. People often don't understand themselves. What they actually need. What they're afraid of. What they really want to say but don't.

"That's when I understood what kind of translation actually matters."

Based in Berlin, working online with clients worldwide. I work in English, Russian, Ukrainian, French, and German.

Ask me directly
  • Coaching is present and future. It's goal-oriented. We work on where you are now and where you want to go — not on what happened to you in the past. Therapy heals the past; coaching builds the future. There's a story about a child who grew up without a bike. As an adult they buy the biggest Lamborghini — but inside, they're still the kid without a bike. To heal that wound: therapy. But if you already have the Lamborghini and simply want to learn to ride a bike now — that's coaching. If you're unsure which one you need, we can talk about it in the intro call.
  • Good. Most of my clients don't — not a clear one anyway. They come with a feeling. Something is off. Something is heavy. Something needs to change but they can't name it yet.
