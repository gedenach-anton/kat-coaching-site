2. Overview and legal bases

I process personal data only as far as this is necessary to run this website, answer enquiries, arrange appointments and deliver my services (coaching, mediation, facilitation, workshops, community). The legal bases are:

  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent), for example for recording a session. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future.
  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (contract and pre-contractual steps), for example for booking an appointment and answering your enquiry.
  • Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation), for example tax and commercial retention duties.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests), for example for the secure operation of the website and protection against abuse.

Please do not send me health data or other particularly sensitive information (Art. 9 GDPR) through this website, the contact form or the booking questions. The content of coaching and mediation conversations is not collected through this website. I will inform you separately at the start of our work together. The confidentiality of our conversations is not affected.

If you nevertheless choose to share such information, I will process it only to answer your enquiry and on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).

In a mediation or workshop I may also learn data about other participants. I will inform those participants about the processing at the start of the respective process (Art. 14 GDPR).

3. Website hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by IONOS SE, Elgendorfer Str. 57, 56410 Montabaur, Germany.

When you visit the website, IONOS processes technically necessary data (server log files), in particular: anonymised IP address, date and time of access, page or file requested, status code, and browser and system information. This serves to deliver the website and to keep it stable and secure. I do not analyse this data myself.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest is the secure and trouble-free operation of the website.

Data processing agreement: A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with IONOS (see also section 9).

Retention: IONOS determines how long log files are kept. For more information see IONOS's privacy policy.

4. No cookies, no tracking, local fonts

This website sets no cookies and places no information on your device (for example in local storage). It uses no analytics, tracking or advertising services, no social media plugins and no embedded third-party content such as videos or maps. No third-party CAPTCHA service is used either.

The fonts (Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans) are stored on this website's own server. No connection to Google Fonts or other font services is made.

Because there is no access to your device that is not strictly necessary, no consent and no cookie banner are required (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

5. Contact by email, phone and contact form

If you contact me by email or phone, I process the data you provide (for example name, email address or phone number, content of your message) to answer your enquiry.

Contact form: The form on the "Contact" page does not send your input to a server of this website. When you submit it, your email program opens with a prepared message (name, email address, interest, message). Your data is only transmitted when you send that email yourself, through your email provider to my mailbox (see section 9). The form also contains a field that is invisible to visitors and detects automated spam submissions. No data is sent to third parties for this.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR if your enquiry relates to a contract or its preparation, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (my legitimate interest in answering enquiries).

Retention: I delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with and no legal retention duties apply. If a contract is concluded, tax retention periods of six to eight years, depending on the document, may apply to contract-related records (§ 147 AO).

6. Appointment booking with Cal.com

The booking buttons on the "Contact" page and elsewhere (for example "Book a free strategic session", "Book a mediation intro call" and "Book a quick call") lead to a booking page at Cal.com. It opens in a new browser window. Data is transmitted to Cal.com only once you open that page. Cal.com is not embedded in my website itself. If JavaScript is disabled in your browser, your email program opens instead.

Provider: Cal.com, Inc., 2261 Market Street #4382, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA. For bookings made through my link, Cal.com states that it processes your data as a processor on my instructions.

Data processed: name, email address, your answers to the booking questions (for example your topic and preferred language), the chosen appointment and time zone, and technical data such as IP address, browser and device information and timestamps. Cal.com may use technically necessary cookies on the booking page. Details are in Cal.com's privacy policy.

Purpose and legal basis: arranging and managing appointments at your request, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, and the secure operation of the booking, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Third countries: Cal.com processes data in the USA and states that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and uses standard contractual clauses where needed.

Data processing agreement: A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Cal.com.

Retention: If no contract is concluded, I delete booking data at the latest six months after the appointment. Otherwise I delete it when our work together ends, unless statutory retention duties require otherwise.

Please do not give details about your health or other particularly sensitive matters in the booking questions.

Retention: I do not store the content of calls. Zoom stores connection and metadata under its own rules.

If you prefer not to use Zoom, please tell me before the appointment and we will find an alternative together.

15. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • access to your stored data (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR)

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing of your data that is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I will then stop processing the data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

To exercise your rights, write to me at info@grodska.com.

Right to lodge a complaint (Art. 77 GDPR): You may complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The authority responsible for my place of business is the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany, www.datenschutz-berlin.de.

16. Providing data, automated decisions

You can use the website without providing personal data. You are neither legally nor contractually obliged to provide data to me. To book online, however, I need at least your name and email address, and to reply by email I need your email address. Without them I cannot process your booking or enquiry. There is no automated decision-making, including profiling, under Art. 22 GDPR.

17. Data security and encryption

For security reasons this website uses TLS encryption (HTTPS). You can recognise it by the lock symbol in your browser's address bar. The connections to Cal.com and Zoom are encrypted as well.

18. Updates to this policy

I will update this privacy policy when the website, the services used or the legal situation change. The version published here applies.
