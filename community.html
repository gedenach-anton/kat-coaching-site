Community and workshops for individuals

Open to everyoneWorkshops and trainings

I regularly run workshops and trainings, online and in Berlin. Topics range widely, from money and career to relationships and emotions, among others. I share what I've learned in years of coaching, facilitation, and my own trial and error, and I turn it into practice, not lectures. You work on your own question from the first minute.

In a group, you see that you're not alone with what you're dealing with. You meet like-minded people, see how others handle what you're in right now, and get different perspectives on the same situation.

How to join

To find out what the next workshop is about, where and when, write to me via the contact form or check @grodska_coaching on Instagram.

For people building somethingCommunity for founders

I host a community for people building their own business, from an idea or a plan on paper to the first paying clients. Some are still employed, some have already made the jump. What they share is a business that's becoming real, and the step into leading it.

Each session has a facilitated topic, followed by peer exchange: what's working, where you're stuck, what you're trying next.

Where
Online, in English
Session
120 minutes
Group
Small groups of 6 to 8 participants
Membership

How to join

To join the community, write to me via the contact form.
